What I took away from it

  

  
    
MEDIUM1 Jun 2026 · 4 min read

    
Inside Google I/O 2026: What Changed My Mind About the Future of AI

    
“The conversation wasn’t centered on prompts. It was centered on orchestration.”

    

      I went expecting model announcements and left convinced the hard part is
      everything around the model — evaluation, tool use, latency, recovery,
      deployment. The shift from “how do we get a better answer?” to
      “how do we build systems that consistently achieve outcomes?”
      is why everything I build now is agentic.
    

    
“Access is no longer the differentiator. Execution is.”
